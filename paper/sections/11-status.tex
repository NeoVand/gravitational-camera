\section{Status of this draft}\label{sec:status}

This is a working draft, published as it is written. Still to come:
\begin{itemize}
\item a worked near-field inversion of the room scenes, from simulated pixel
readings to an image, and the same limit for an array that does not face the
scene flat on;
\item the wave-zone theory for a general source;
\item the single bound relating focusing, reflection, absorption and shadow to
compactness;
\item the emission of real materials, whose friction depends on frequency,
and the comparison of surface with volume emission;
\item how well waves can be separated from nearby pull in the presence of
noise, and the limits of the three-dimensional reconstructions of
Section~\ref{sec:separation};
\item the full magnetic kernel behind Eq.~\eqref{eq:current-column}, which we
have argued for but not yet written out.
\end{itemize}
The comparison with earlier work is also unfinished. Several papers that bear
directly on Sections~\ref{sec:thermal} and~\ref{sec:no-lens} have so far been
read only in abstract.
